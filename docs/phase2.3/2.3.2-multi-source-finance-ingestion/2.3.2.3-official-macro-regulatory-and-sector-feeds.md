# 2.3.2.3 — Official Macro, Regulatory, and Sector Feeds

## Objective

Add first-party economic, policy, and sector events that improve company and
market research beyond ticker-specific news.

## Feed groups

### Macro and rates

- Federal Reserve RSS: policy statements, minutes, speeches, regulation, and
  statistical releases;
- US Treasury: daily nominal/real yield and bill-rate feeds;
- BLS: CPI, PPI, employment, wages, and release metadata;
- BEA: GDP, corporate profits, income, and trade;
- EIA: energy prices, production, inventories, and generation;
- New York Fed: SOFR/EFFR and market-operation data;
- CFTC: weekly Commitments of Traders positioning.

### Sector/regulatory

- openFDA for recalls, safety events, and shortages relevant to healthcare;
- NHTSA recalls/investigations relevant to automotive and transport;
- USAspending awards relevant to government contractors.

Source URLs, release dates, revision/vintage fields, and official identifiers are
mandatory provenance. FRED remains a normalized macro source; direct official
feeds add releases and context rather than blindly duplicating FRED series.

## Step 1: Build feed adapters behind one contract

Create one module per agency inside `src/ingestion/official/`, sharing only small
HTTP/date/pagination helpers. Each adapter returns normalized observations or
narrative records. Avoid a single giant conditional ingestor.

RSS/XML/CSV/JSON differences stay inside adapters. Network retries stay in the
scheduler/resilience layer except for provider-directed pagination.

## Step 2: Add a curated series/release catalog

Configuration explicitly lists enabled series and document feeds. Do not crawl
every available government dataset.

Catalog entries include:

- stable internal metric/event name;
- agency dataset/series identifier;
- unit/frequency and expected release cadence;
- source category and applicable sectors;
- whether the payload is structured, narrative, or both.

## Step 3: Map sector events conservatively

Agency records first map to issuer aliases, manufacturer names, recipient UEIs,
or other exact identifiers stored in the security registry. A fuzzy candidate may
be recorded for review but must not automatically attach evidence to a ticker.

One event may attach to multiple securities. The original agency identity remains
the deduplication authority.

## Step 4: Preserve revisions and vintages

Macro data is revisable. Upserts retain the source's observation period and
vintage/retrieval time so the RAG can distinguish "latest revised value" from
"value known on a historical date." Do not overwrite a historical vintage when
the source exposes revisions.

## Testing

- Parse one representative fixture per response format and agency group.
- Retain release/vintage metadata and units across revisions.
- Prove a revised observation does not destroy an earlier vintage.
- Map exact issuer/recipient/manufacturer aliases and leave ambiguous names
  unattached.
- Disable a missing-key agency independently while no-key feeds continue.
- Replay every fixture idempotently.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Only curated datasets/releases are ingested
- [ ] Structured observations and narrative releases use the correct store
- [ ] Official identifiers and URLs remain primary provenance
- [ ] Revisions/vintages are queryable and not silently overwritten
- [ ] Sector mappings require exact identifiers or explicit review
- [ ] One agency failure cannot block another agency or company source
